## Destination

DocuBite's authenticated app works the way Vic.ai's does, end to end: **Vic's interaction grammar, DocuBite's skin.** Every operator surface — Invoices, Purchase Orders, Receipts, Bank Statements, Exceptions, Approvals (Invoices / PO Mismatches), Bill Pay, Payment Batches, and Admin — shares one shell: a full-width 62px queue, one header band (View · Sort · summary filter chips opening a hierarchical facet panel), a bulk action row, and one right-hand Detail pane with a sticky bottom action bar. PO mismatches are resolved on the invoice row with per-cell match glyphs and the tolerance arithmetic in view. Payment runs are batched, approved and exported without DocuBite moving money. Admin is a distinct area (Configuration, Approval Flows, Mismatch Flows, Users, Companies) with multi-company access. Approval works on a phone. Implemented and shipped, measured by Impeccable's in-page detector and a critique score above the 20/40 baseline on every queue.

Evidence: the full self-guided tour walked 2026-09-15 — `docs/vic-ai-ux-tour-findings.md` §6 and the screenshots in `docs/vic-ai-tour-2026-09-15/` (DocuBite's own Invoices screen from the same day sits beside them).

**Supersedes [Map: Vic.ai-style typed AP surfaces (#177)](https://github.com/petrose99/docubite/issues/177).** #177's thirty closed decisions are this map's baseline and are cited, not re-decided. **Why a second map.** #177 reached its destination as feature slices bolted onto the old pages, with no ticket owning composition and no in-browser verification, and scored 20/40 ([#224](https://github.com/petrose99/docubite/issues/224)). This map charts by *surface*, each ticket owning a whole screen's shape, and every execution ticket closes on detector counts.

**Execution mode.** This map carries execution. Decision tickets resolve first; each spawns an execution ticket; execution tickets close only with `impeccable` run at the four points in CLAUDE.md, in-page detector before/after counts at 1440 and 390 (list and pane-open), and `polish`. Dev-server + seed + headless-Chromium recipe is on #225.

**Design protocol.** Every rendered-surface ticket calls the Skill tool for `impeccable` (sub-command named on the ticket) and `intent`, before the work. Grilling tickets on a surface open with `impeccable critique` of the incumbent and cite the tour screenshot they are matching.

**Vic wins ties, with the tour as the record.** "Does this follow Vic or DocuBite?" defaults to Vic without asking, and the answer is read off `docs/vic-ai-ux-tour-findings.md` §6 and the screenshots — not from memory of the older sections. Grill only what the tour does not settle: DocuBite-only features (Exceptions, Touchless, does-not-move-money, bank statements, institutions), data DocuBite lacks (payment terms, discounts, buyers, entities), and anything that would reverse a #177 decision.

**Documented exceptions to Vic** (decided, do not re-open by "Vic wins ties"): the combined fields+source Detail pane (#179 → #224) with History as its tabs instead of Vic's left panel; the collapsible icon rail instead of Vic's top bar (#224 Q5); DocuBite's palette, type and emerald brand (this map, Q2); Exceptions as its own destination (#192); Touchless keeps its name.

**Settled while charting (grilled 2026-09-15):**

- **Scope is the whole operator app** — queues + Detail pane, Approvals inbox, PO matching in the row, Bill Pay + Payment Batches, Admin, mobile approval. One grammar everywhere.
- **Fidelity is grammar, not skin.** Same layouts, panels, chips, bars, keyboard flow, copy patterns; colour and type stay DocuBite's.
- **Payments never move money.** Bill Pay queue (open bills, terms, countdown, discounted amount over struck-through due) and Payment Batches queue (batch → approve → export file). The transfer stays outside DocuBite, as `preparePaymentRun` does today.
- **Approvals is a destination with two queues**, Invoices and PO Mismatches, each a personal Ready-to-Approve view with the sticky Approve/Reject bar. Exceptions stays separate.
- **Admin adopts Vic's full IA including Companies**: Configuration (per-field Editable/Required/Width, consequence toggles), Approval Flows, PO Mismatch Flows, Users, Companies — one login, many companies, per-entity permissions. This is the one schema-and-auth effort on the map; it is researched before it is grilled.
- **Build order:** shell (#225) → distill (#238) → Approvals → PO matching → Payments → Admin → Mobile.
- **Removal protocol (owner's rule, #237):** nothing is removed from the app without the owner signing off each removal by name on the ticket. Removed features are *unplugged* (CONTEXT.md term), not deleted, so a return is a re-link.
