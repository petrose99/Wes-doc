# ADR-001: Bigcapital is DocuBite's internal ledger

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: Superseded 2026-09-22 (#380, map #376) — Bigcapital's data was ruled
demo-only and the whole integration was deleted; DocuBite hosts no ledger of
its own. Kept as a historical record; do not build against it.

## Decision

The self-hosted, white-labeled Bigcapital instance is DocuBite's internal
double-entry ledger. Bigcapital is auto-provisioned per workspace on creation
(`models/bigcapital.ts`, enqueued from `models/workspaces.ts`), themed as
DocuBite (`bigcapital/` assets), and reached from the app via SSO handoff
(`app/api/accounting/session/route.ts`). Bigcapital owns debits/credits, the
chart of accounts, and financial reports (trial balance, general ledger, and
AP/AR aging already flow into sheets via `reportTableToSheet`).

DocuBite does not duplicate a journal in Prisma. Its Prisma models are the
**automation layer on top** of the ledger: documents, GL coding, matching,
approvals, and a `LedgerTransaction` mirror used only for fast local queries
against the ledger (never the source of truth).

QuickBooks Online and Xero remain **optional external connectors** for
customers who bring their own books. They are addressed through the same
`IntegrationPush` machinery as Bigcapital and never own the ledger for a
workspace that has not opted in.

## Consequences

- Automation outcomes (bills, expenses, invoices, payments, reconciliation)
  are posted **into Bigcapital as first-class writes** — not best-effort
  pushes and not maintained only in Prisma.
- Reconciliation state is authoritative in Bigcapital plus its mirror row
  in `LedgerTransaction` (with `reconciledSource` recording which side won a
  particular row: `docubite` when DocuBite closed the loop, `provider` when
  the ledger reported it back).
- New financial reports do not live in DocuBite. If a customer needs a P&L,
  cash flow, or aging report, it is generated by Bigcapital and surfaced.
- The deprecated Univer sheet
  (`components/sheet/README.md` schedules deletion) is not built on. Read
  models against the ledger use the workspace sheet pipeline against
  Bigcapital reports where a sheet view is required.

## Not chosen

- **A native DocuBite journal.** Rejected. It would require GAAP-compliant
  double-entry semantics, chart-of-accounts management, closing-period
  guarantees, and financial reporting that Bigcapital already ships. The
  DocuBite team owns automation, not ledger primitives.
- **QuickBooks or Xero as the default ledger.** Rejected because we can't
  guarantee every customer has an account, and because self-hosted keeps
  data inside DocuBite's HIPAA boundary. QBO/Xero remain first-class
  connectors when a customer's books already live there.
