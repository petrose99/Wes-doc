## Resolution

One active Payout destination per Payee (bank or wallet, never several, never a default among several) — matches the singular "acknowledged details" shape ADR 0006 already used and avoids a default-selection UI nothing in the ticket asked for.

**Shape** (added to CONTEXT.md as *Payout destination*, and to ADR 0006's Consequences):
- Bank: `bankName` (Standard Lesotho Bank | FNB Lesotho | Nedbank Lesotho), `branchCode`, `accountNumber`.
- Wallet: `network` (C-Pay | M-Pesa | EcoCash | MyWallet | Khetsi — the five from #422's research), `number`, `accountName`.
- Both validated only in form (presence, length, the digits a bank/network expects); DocuBite verifies neither. A bank destination is modelled and payable regardless of whether Chaperone happens to carry the route — going live on a bank route is a credentials switch (map #421 Notes), not a model change.

**ADR 0006 widened** (renamed *Bank details are saved only by people…* → *Payout details are saved only by people…*, `docs/adr/0006-...md`): every rule (holds the Payee, acknowledge/reject, restores on reject, snapshotted at submit, self-acknowledged) applies identically to a switch between bank and wallet as to an edit within one kind.

**"First details are not a change" and the wallet→bank case:** still holds only for the very first destination a Payee ever gets. Because there is one destination per Payee, a bank added after a wallet already exists (or the reverse) *replaces* the wallet — there's nothing to add beside it — so it's a Payout details change like any other, not a second first-sight exemption.

**The invoice Check:** "details differ from those on file" now fails on a **kind mismatch alone** (file holds a wallet, invoice shows a bank account, or the reverse) before any field is compared — a kind switch is the sharpest possible "these don't match."

**Glossary:** renamed *Bank details change* → *Payout details change* throughout CONTEXT.md (the Bill Pay and Payment batch entries already used the new name; the definition entry itself lagged) and widened its body to cover both kinds and a kind switch. Also touched: *Bank confirmation* (now "Payout destination" in its body), *Bill Pay* (eligibility-reason wording).

No design-skill pass: this ticket touches only `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/`, no `app/**` or `components/**` — CLAUDE.md's path-based gate does not apply, and no rendered surface exists yet to critique.
