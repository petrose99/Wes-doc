## Decisions recorded
v3.1 through Nango's `sage` provider does not reach South African Sage Business Cloud Accounting businesses; this is a platform/product split, not a scopes or auth gap. The ZA v2.0.0 API (`accounting.sageone.co.za/api/2.0.0`) does reach them, but only through its own auth, outside Nango entirely. #384 and #383 stand unchanged (both are correct for v3.1's real countries). #387 stands for v3.1 but must not grow ZA support before #438 resolves the scope question.
