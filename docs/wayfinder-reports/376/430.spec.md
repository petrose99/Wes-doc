# Spec — #430 Build: correcting posted bills' Accounts in the ledger

Kind: surface + backend. Decided in *Guessed ledger defaults* (#402, ADR 0011). Builds directly on
#429 (`SupplierAccountRule`, `IntegrationConnection.defaultExpenseAccount*`, `Document.codingData`
per-line `account_external_id`/`account_source`). Providers: QuickBooks and Xero only — Sage
correction is out of scope here (lands with the Sage provider build). Research:
`docs/research/ledger-bill-account-update.md`.

## Problem & user need

An Owner or bookkeeper fixes a wrong Supplier rule or Default account after bills have already
posted to the wrong ledger account. Nothing un-posts automatically (ADR 0011) — today those bills
sit silently wrong in the ledger with no path back except editing them by hand in QuickBooks/Xero.
This ticket gives DocuBite a bounded, ledger-safe way to push the correction back: from the rule
that changed (bulk, list path) or from one bill in the Detail pane (single-row path).

## Design approach

- **Account only, nothing else.** Amounts, VAT and tax type are never touched — this is a
  bookkeeping correction, not a re-edit. Every line is resent because both providers replace
  the whole line set on update (Xero deletes omitted lines; QBO full-update nulls omitted fields) —
  DocuBite reads the bill fresh immediately before sending so no other field regresses.
- **DocuBite follows the ledger's own locks, invents none.** QBO `BookCloseDate`, Xero status +
  `AmountPaid` + lock dates are checked before a row is offered, not guessed at.
- **Paid bills are offered where the ledger allows** (Xero: yes, with AccountCode explicitly on
  Xero's editable-on-paid field list; QBO: unverified per research — pre-check treats a paid QBO
  bill as refusable-unless-proven-otherwise, see Engineering Questions).
- **What we did NOT do:** no bulk "correct everything at once across rules" screen — one rule's
  posted bills at a time, from where the rule was changed. No retroactive VAT/tax-type change. No
  Sage in this ticket.

## UX questions answered

- How does the Owner learn there's something to fix? The rule's own row, immediately after the
  change, and persistently until resolved.
- What if the Owner doesn't want to fix it now? "Not now" leaves the reminder; "Leave them"
  dismisses it for good, per bill or per batch.
- What if the ledger refuses one bill and not another? Partial outcome, one row's result at a time.
- What if the token is stale? Re-read once, retry once, then fail that row with a reason.

## Ethical review

- No forced choice: "Not now" and "Leave them" are always available and are not styled to lose to
  the primary action.
- No dark pattern in the bulk selector: the header checkbox selects only rows the ledger allows;
  it never pre-ticks by default (a silent bulk-write is the anti-pattern this avoids — Intent
  catalog, "forced continuity" family, mis-applied here as "forced bulk action").
- Reason text for an un-offerable bill is in plain words ("Paid in Xero — account can't be changed
  through DocuBite"), never a provider error code.

## Design specification

### Screen 1 — List path: "N bills already posted" dialog

**Intent.** Fires the moment a Supplier account rule or the connection Default is changed and the
system finds Posted (or Paid, where the ledger allows) bills still on the old account. Without it,
a rule change silently stops fixing future bills only — the Owner would have no idea past bills
are still wrong.

**Trigger.** On save of a new/changed `SupplierAccountRule` mapping or `IntegrationConnection`
Default, server-side: find bills whose `Document.codingData.items[].account_external_id` matches
the *old* resolved account and whose ledger fact (`lib/documents/stages.ts` derivation) is `posted`
or `paid`, for that connection. If the count is 0, no dialog — nothing to show.

**Layout.** A modal (interruption is warranted: it follows a state-changing save and needs a
decision before the Owner moves on) titled "3 bills already posted to Sundry Expenses" (account
name interpolated from the *old* resolved account, count from the query). Below: a table, one row
per affected bill —

| ☐ | Supplier | Date | Amount | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Acme Fuels | 12 Sep | R 1,240.00 | 2 lines → Fuel |

- Header checkbox selects/deselects every row the ledger currently allows (see states below);
  it is never pre-checked.
- "Change" column: "N lines → {new account name}" — the account the rule now resolves to.
- Rows the ledger refuses sort to the bottom, checkbox disabled, "Change" column replaced by the
  refusal reason in words plus an "Open in ledger" link (deep link to the provider's own edit
  screen using the stored `externalBillId`).
- Row for a Paid bill (where offered) carries a small "Paid" tag before the supplier name.

**Copy.**
- Title: "{N} bills already posted to {old account name}"
- Body intro (once, above the table): "Update the Account on these to {new account name}? Amounts
  and VAT stay as posted."
- Primary button: "Update {N} bills in {Provider}" (N = current selection count; QuickBooks or
  Xero named, never "the ledger"). Disabled, not hidden, at 0 selected.
- Secondary button: "Not now".
- Refusal reason examples: "Books closed for this period — update in QuickBooks", "Locked period in
  Xero", "Paid in QuickBooks — account can't be changed through DocuBite" (only shown for the
  provider where paid is in fact refused).

**Interaction logic.**
- On load: query runs server-side before the dialog renders (no client-side loading spinner inside
  the dialog for the initial list — it opens already populated, since it's triggered by a save the
  Owner is already waiting on).
- Header checkbox: selects all rows whose refusal reason is empty.
- "Update N bills in {Provider}": submits selected rows. Per-row result comes back as the write
  completes (see Outcome below) — this is not optimistic; each row waits for its own
  server-confirmed write.
- "Not now": closes the dialog, leaves every affected bill's reminder in place (see Screen 3).
- Escape / click-outside: same as "Not now".

**Outcome (Partial).** After submit, the dialog switches from selection to result: each
previously-selected row shows a check ("Updated") or an X with the failure reason inline (same
words as a pre-listed refusal, plus "Stale — try again" for the one-retry-then-fail case). A footer
line: "{updated} of {selected} updated." Close button reads "Done" once every row has resolved.
Rows still unresolved keep the reminder (Screen 3) exactly as if they had never been selected.

**Accessibility.** Dialog is a native `<dialog>`/Radix Dialog with focus trapped and returned to the
triggering control on close. Header checkbox and each row checkbox are real `<input
type="checkbox">` with a visible label (supplier name), not icon-only. Refusal reason is in the
accessible name of the disabled checkbox's row, not color alone (a muted row plus text, never
color-only greying).

**States.** Default (as above) · all rows refused (table with zero enabled checkboxes, primary
button permanently disabled, body copy stays factual — no dead-end without the "Open in ledger"
escape on every row) · loading during submit (row-level, not full-dialog) · partial outcome
(above) · empty (0 affected bills — dialog never opens, no state to design).

### Screen 2 — Single-row path: Detail pane Account edit on a Posted/Paid row

**Intent.** Lets a person fix one bill without waiting for or triggering a rule change — the
narrow, immediate correction when only one document is wrong.

**Behavior.** On a document whose ledger fact is `posted` or `paid` (`lib/documents/stages.ts`
derivation, `app/.../documents/[documentId]/page.tsx:243`), the Detail pane's line-items editor
(`components/documents/line-items-editor.tsx`) keeps only the **Account** field editable per line;
every other field (amount, tax type, description, quantity) renders read-only, matching the
existing read-only treatment used elsewhere in that component for locked fields.

**Interaction logic.**
- Editing any line's Account marks the document "Account change pending" (local state, not yet
  written) — an unsaved-change affordance already used elsewhere in this pane (dirty-state border
  or dot) applies here too.
- A bar/button appears once at least one line's Account differs from the posted value: "Update in
  {Provider}". Below it, the same account-only sentence as the list dialog: "Only the account
  changes; amounts and VAT stay as posted."
- Click "Update in {Provider}": server round-trip using the same account-only resend path as the
  list dialog (read the bill fresh, keep every other field, tax type resent unchanged, current
  `SyncToken`/version). Not optimistic — the button shows a brief in-place loading state ("Updating
  …") and only commits the visual change to "Posted" (no pending marker) on server confirmation.
- On failure: the edited Account reverts is **not** what happens — the person's edit stays in the
  field (never clear user input, per `fortify`), and an inline error appears under the "Update in
  {Provider}" button with the same plain-words reason vocabulary as Screen 1, plus a retry.
- **This path never teaches the Supplier rule.** No `SupplierAccountRule` write happens here — it's
  a one-bill fix, explicitly scoped in the ticket's question. If the person wants future bills for
  that supplier to use the new account too, they go set the rule (existing #429 surface); nothing
  on this screen implies otherwise (copy avoids "always" language).

**Copy.**
- Update button: "Update in QuickBooks" / "Update in Xero" (provider named, matches Screen 1).
- Helper sentence under the button: "Only the account changes; amounts and VAT stay as posted."
- Error (locked period): "Books closed for this period — update in {Provider} directly."
- Error (paid, refused): "Paid in {Provider} — this line can't be changed through DocuBite."
- Error (stale token, both retries failed): "Something changed in {Provider} first — reload and try
  again."
- Success: no banner needed — the field simply stops showing the "pending" affordance; the pane's
  existing "Posted"/"Paid" status pill is the confirmation (per `articulate`: don't add a message
  where the state change already reads as the confirmation).

**Accessibility.** The Account `<select>` for a locked line is `aria-readonly` styled identically to
other read-only fields in this editor (existing pattern — reuse, don't invent a second one). The
pending-change affordance is not color-only: an "(unsaved)" visually-hidden suffix on the changed
field's accessible name, consistent with `include`'s standing rule for this pane.

**States.** Default (all lines read-only, Account editable) · dirty (one or more Accounts changed,
"Update in {Provider}" visible) · loading (per-click, button shows "Updating…", disabled) · error
(above, field keeps the edit) · success (pending affordance clears) · locked (Account itself
disabled with inline reason, matching the list dialog's refusal-reason wording, when the pre-check
already knows this bill can't be changed before the person even edits it — cheaper than letting
them try and fail).

### Screen 3 — Rule row reminder: "N posted bills still on … · Review"

**Intent.** Not now must not mean forgotten — this is the standing signal that Screen 1's decision
is still outstanding.

**Layout.** On the Supplier accounts table / Default row (`components/settings/supplier-accounts-
table.tsx`) where the rule that changed lives, a line beneath the rule's own row: "{N} posted bills
still on {old account name}" with a "Review" link/button that reopens Screen 1 with the same query,
re-run fresh (a bill fixed by hand in the ledger meanwhile simply won't be found by the query, so
it silently drops off — no separate reconciliation needed).

**Interaction logic.** Persists until either every affected bill is updated (list shrinks to 0 and
the line disappears) or the Owner clicks "Leave them" (Screen 1's dialog, reachable a second way:
a small "Leave them" text action next to "Review" on this row, for when they've already decided
without reopening the dialog). "Leave them" is a real decision, not silent — it marks those specific
bills as resolved-without-updating (a boolean/flag per affected `Document`, not a rule-level
setting, so a *future* posted bill on the same wrong account after a *further* rule change still
surfaces fresh).

**Copy.** "{N} posted bills still on {old account name} · Review" — "Leave them" as the secondary
action, matching Screen 1's own button wording exactly (same word, same meaning, in both places).

**States.** Hidden (0 affected) · shown (N ≥ 1) · after "Leave them" (line disappears immediately,
no confirmation dialog needed — it's non-destructive to ledger data, only dismisses a DocuBite
reminder, and is itself reachable again if the same rule changes again).

## Use cases

1. **Bookkeeper corrects a wrong Supplier rule for a vendor with 3 posted Xero bills, 2 unpaid, 1
   paid.** All 3 rows offered (Xero allows AccountCode on paid). Ticks all 3, clicks "Update 3
   bills in Xero". Server confirms all 3; dialog shows 3 checks, "Done".
2. **Owner changes the connection Default; one of the affected QBO bills falls before
   `BookCloseDate`.** That row sorts to the bottom, checkbox disabled, reason "Books closed for
   this period — update in QuickBooks", with "Open in ledger". The other rows still update
   normally. The closed-period bill's reminder stays on the rule row afterward (1 posted bill still
   on the old account) until the books reopen or the Owner clicks "Leave them".
3. **A single Xero bill was miscoded by mistake (not a rule problem) — the Owner fixes it in the
   Detail pane.** Opens the document, changes the one line's Account, clicks "Update in Xero". No
   rule is touched; the next bill from the same supplier still resolves however the existing rule
   says.
4. **Stale SyncToken on a QBO bill (someone edited it in QuickBooks between listing and updating).**
   DocuBite re-reads the bill once, retries the update once with the fresh token. If that also
   fails, the row shows "Something changed in QuickBooks first — reload and try again," and stays
   in the reminder count.
5. **Not now, then never revisited.** Owner clicks "Not now" at Screen 1. The rule row keeps "N
   posted bills still on … · Review" indefinitely (no auto-expiry) until acted on.

## Engineering questions

- **Open in sandbox (named in the ticket):** whether a QuickBooks paid bill keeps its payment link
  after an account-only full update. Until verified, the QBO pre-check treats a paid bill as
  refused by default (conservative) — flip to offered once the sandbox confirms the link survives.
- No `syncToken` is persisted anywhere today (`lib/integrations/quickbooks/client.ts` fetches it
  live via `getBillRef`, only currently called from `voidBill`); the correction path reuses that
  live-fetch-then-write pattern rather than adding a cached column, to avoid a second source of
  truth that can itself go stale.
- Xero has no SyncToken-equivalent optimistic-concurrency token in this research pass — the
  "re-read and retry once" rule applies to both providers uniformly at the *line-set* level (resend
  every current line, not just the changed one) rather than a token compare for Xero.

## Test plan

- **Engineering:** the account-only resend never mutates amounts/tax on a bill the pre-check
  offers; a bill the pre-check refuses is never listed as selectable; a write-time refusal (pre-
  check said yes, provider said no between read and write) lands the row in the same Partial-outcome
  UI, not a thrown error.
- **Design/Owner:** an Owner who changes a Supplier rule with posted bills outstanding can, without
  documentation, find and clear the reminder within one session.

## Pending questions

- Sandbox verification named above (QBO paid-bill payment-link survival) — does not block building
  the surface; the pre-check defaults conservatively until it's answered.
