## Decisions recorded
QuickBooks Online (`quickbooks`), Xero (`xero`) and Sage Business Cloud Accounting (`sage`) are all ready OAUTH2 providers in Nango's catalogue for an auth + proxy integration. Exact scopes to configure: QuickBooks `com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting`; Xero `offline_access openid accounting.transactions accounting.contacts accounting.settings`; Sage `full_access`. Tenant/business id: QuickBooks' `realmId` comes off the OAuth callback via `redirect_uri_metadata`; Xero's multi-organisation pick is served by Nango's `xeroPostConnection` hook + `get-tenants` action (replaces the app's own `fetchConnections` call); Sage has no templated tenant field — DocuBite's connect flow must call `GET /businesses` itself after connecting. Attachment endpoints and the full Sage bill-mapper endpoint list (no Sage client exists yet) are recorded in the resolution comment. Sandboxes for all three are self-serve via each vendor's own developer portal, feeding the HITL provisioning ticket.
