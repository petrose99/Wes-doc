## Decisions recorded
No direct bank feed reaches any Lesotho bank (Standard Lesotho Bank, FNB Lesotho, Nedbank Lesotho, Lesotho PostBank) on QuickBooks Online, Xero, or Sage — confirmed absent from each vendor's own supported-bank list. SA coverage is a thin patchwork (QBO: 6 named banks; Xero: dedicated direct-feed articles only for Investec and Nedbank; Sage's claim of 5 majors unverified — page blocked). No aggregator (Salt Edge, Stitch, Mono, Okra) confirmed to reach Lesotho. Absent a feed, all three ledgers natively accept manual CSV/OFX/QBO/QFX import — solved, not a gap. All three APIs let a caller create a bank transaction (Xero `BankTransactions`, QBO `Purchase`/`Deposit`/`BankTransfer`, Sage `bank_transactions`), but these land as ordinary recorded transactions, not unreconciled statement lines in a "for review" queue — that path is partner-only (Xero Bank Feeds API) or unexposed (QBO, Sage). Conclusion: DocuBite's own Bank Statement posting fills a real gap for Lesotho but can't replicate a native feed's reconcile-queue experience on any provider.
