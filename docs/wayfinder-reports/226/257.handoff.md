  (`approvals/invoices/page.tsx` — `scopeToMe`/`onlyNotEligible`). To make the Filter sheet's

  pages pass `allRows`; the two queue components supply the predicate. `poMismatchCount` /
  `invoiceCount` for the segments then also come from the unfiltered sets filtered by the same
  predicate.

  snapshot of the decided row (`decided` state) and passes it to `QueueScreen` as a pinned row

  open on it with the strip instead of #249's close-with-toast; desktop additionally keeps its
  toast. `PaneHelpers` gains `next: (() => void) | null` for "Next to approve".

- Sort labels to the spec's copy matrix: Invoices "Waiting longest first" · "Amount, high to
