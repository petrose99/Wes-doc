## Context established (Intent)

- **Users.** The *Claimant*: a member who paid for something personally and wants the money back — usually a Reviewer on the desktop Receipts lane, sometimes on the phone. The *payer*: whoever runs Bill Pay (any member submits a batch, an owner approves and uploads the file, #229 decision 3). The *Approver* decided the claim already; this ticket starts after Approved.
- **Product.** Bill Pay + Payment Batches shipped on #251; expense claims shipped on #273. Today an approved claim ends with "‹Claimant› is owed ‹amount›" (the approve confirm's own words, `components/queue/expense-claim-queue.tsx:137`) and nothing follows — a Dead End (catalog cat. 9).
- **Constraints.** DocuBite never moves money (#229). `PaymentRunItem` is document-keyed; `InvoicePayment` is invoice-keyed; `WorkspaceMember` holds no bank details; the ZA EFT CSV is payee-agnostic (`beneficiary_name, account_number, branch_code, amount, currency, reference…`, `lib/payments/za-eft-csv.ts:53`). A claim is single-currency by construction (`expense_claim_currency_mismatch`, `models/expense-claims.ts:45`).
- **Ethical stance.** A person's bank account is personal data: minimum collection, workspace-scoped, shown masked, written only into the file the bank needs, changeable by its owner, audited.
- **Vic.** The tour is AP-only; Vic has no reimbursements, so "Vic wins ties" cannot settle this. Precedents: #229 (payments grammar), #247 (claims), ADR 0001 (derived paid state), #233 (one word, one meaning).
- **Mode (Impeccable).** Operate. No incumbent to critique: the approved claim has no next surface today, so there is no score (the map's "score the new UI" rule).

## Grilling rounds (owner's side answered by delegation — every `➡️` taken)

### Round 1 — the fork

❓ **Q1 — Does an approved claim enter Bill Pay at all?** (a) Yes: a Bill Pay row whose payee is the Claimant, batchable like a bill. (b) No: the claim ends at Approved and gains a *Mark as reimbursed* action on the claim itself; the transfer is entirely outside. (c) Hybrid: outside by default, into Bill Pay only once the Claimant has bank details.

➡️ **(a).** The approve confirm already says "‹who› is owed ‹amount›"; a debt the workspace owes with nowhere to pay it from is a Dead End (cat. 9). Bill Pay is defined as *the queue from which payment batches are created* and the payment file is payee-agnostic — nothing in #229 assumes the payee is a company. The objections in the ticket (no Invoice #, no terms, no discount) are *empty columns*, not a different concept, and #229 decision 5 already renders a supplier without terms as "—". (c) is (a) with the row hidden when it is undecidable, which #229 decision 7 explicitly rejected for suppliers ("shown, undecidable, Needs bank details" instead of dropped silently). *Principle: systems over screens; make intent visible.*

---

❓ **Q2 — Where do the Claimant's bank details live?** (a) `WorkspaceMember` (workspace-scoped, one set per membership). (b) `User` (one set across every workspace). (c) A `Supplier` record of kind "person", reusing the supplier bank fields and the existing eligibility path.

➡️ **(a) WorkspaceMember.** Privacy by default: each workspace holds only what it pays; leaving a workspace drops the details with the membership. (b) leaks one company's payroll-ish data into every other workspace the person joins. (c) puts a person in the vendor master, where Invoices' supplier matching, statements and posting would start treating them as a vendor — Inconsistent Patterns (cat. 9) and a real posting risk. *Principle: ethical defaults.*

---

❓ **Q3 — Who enters the details, and are they required to submit a claim?** (a) Required at Submit for approval. (b) Optional, self-service by the Claimant from the Account menu (desktop) and Account tab (phone), also editable by an owner; a claim without details reaches Bill Pay as *Needs bank details*, like a supplier.

➡️ **(b).** Approval is about whether the money is owed, not whether it can be paid yet; gating Submit on bank details would block the Approver on the Claimant's admin. #229 decision 7 already has the undecidable-with-reason row. The owner-editable copy lives in Admin › Users once #286 ships; until then the Claimant's own entry suffices. *Principle: real conditions (the Claimant fills it in when they get to it).*

---

❓ **Q4 — Model: extend `PaymentRunItem` or a parallel claim item?** (a) `PaymentRunItem` gains a nullable `expenseClaimId`, exactly one of `documentId`/`expenseClaimId` set; a claim-side payment record `ExpenseClaimPayment` mirrors `InvoicePayment`. (b) A separate `PaymentRunClaimItem` table and a separate file path.

➡️ **(a).** One batch, one items list, one file writer, one double-batching guard (`(workspaceId, expenseClaimId, active)` unique, the same shape as documents). The payment record is separate because `InvoicePayment` is invoice-keyed and its ledger-confirmation source does not apply to a claim. *Smallest reversible option; domain-modeling: one Payment batch concept, two payee kinds.*

### Round 2 — the row, the batch, the file (Q1 = yes)

❓ **Q5 — What do the Bill Pay columns say for a claim?** The row has no Invoice #, date, terms or discount.

➡️ Column header **Supplier → Payee** on Bill Pay only (glossary: *Payee* = the supplier or the Claimant a row pays); a claim row shows the Claimant's name with a small *Claim* mark beside it, never colour alone. Invoice # → the claim title, else "Expense claim"; Invoice date → submitted date; Terms → "—" and no discount, ever (#229 decision 5's rule applied verbatim); Due → the approval date, shown as a date with **no countdown** (a claim has no terms so nothing is "overdue"; a manufactured countdown is Fabricated Urgency, cat. 3); Bill total → the frozen total; Amount to pay → the frozen total, **read-only** (Q7). Phone card: Payee · Amount · Due, unchanged from #235's slots.

---

❓ **Q6 — Same batch as supplier bills, or claims only in their own batch?**

➡️ **Same batch.** #229 decision 4's invariant is *one batch = one payer account = one currency = one file*; the bank portal does not care who the beneficiary is. A claims-only batch would double the owner's approvals for no reason the file needs. In the batch pane the lines are grouped by payee as today, with claim lines under a **Reimbursements** group after the suppliers so the owner sees at a glance that people are being paid in this file. The file row: `beneficiary_name` = Claimant, `your_reference` / `beneficiary_reference` = "Expense claim ‹title or submitted date›", `beneficiary_email` = the member's email.

---

❓ **Q7 — Partial payment of a claim?** Bills allow 0 < amount ≤ due inline.

➡️ **No.** The claim's amount is the frozen approved total (#247 decision 1: "one frozen amount"); a partial reimbursement would contradict what the Approver approved. Amount to pay is read-only on a claim row; *Mark as paid* by hand still writes a record with the full amount. If partial reimbursement is ever asked for it is a new claim, not a partial payment.

---

❓ **Q8 — How do the claim's processing state and the derived paid state relate?**

➡️ `ExpenseClaim.status` stays draft · submitted · approved · rejected — untouched. Paid state is **derived from claim payment records only** (ADR 0001 applied without its ledger source, because a claim is never reconciled with the ledger): Unpaid → *Scheduled* while held by a pending/approved batch → *Paid (recorded)* once records ≥ total. A claim leaves Bill Pay when it reads Paid. On the receipt's Approval tab and the Expense claims view the Claim pill stays **Approved** (no fifth pill; #233's one-word rule) and the stage timeline gains *Paid · ‹date› · ‹by›* as an event; the Status line reads *In an approved claim · Paid ‹date›*.

---

❓ **Q9 — Who sees the account number?**

➡️ Nobody in the UI: bank name + last four everywhere (the Payer account convention, #229 decision 4); the full number and branch code go only into the payment file. Editable by the Claimant and any owner; every change writes an audit event; removing the details does not touch rows already in a batch (the file was built from the details at export time, as for suppliers).

---

❓ **Q10 — Mark as paid / Remove payment record on claims?**

➡️ Identical to bills: Server-confirmed, no Undo, *Remove payment record* needs a reason (ADR 0001 consequences). One grammar.

### Round 3 — edges

❓ **Q11 — Does the Claimant learn they have been paid?**

➡️ Not in this ticket. The Approval tab shows *Paid ‹date›*; a claimant-facing notice would ride the #246 email grammar and is fog (recorded on the map), not a ticket yet — there is no evidence claimants poll for it.

---

❓ **Q12 — A Claimant who has left the workspace?**

➡️ The claim keeps its `submitterId` (SetNull only if the User is deleted); the Bill Pay row shows *Needs bank details* with the reason *No longer a member*, and an owner can still *Mark as paid* by hand. No new mechanism.

---

❓ **Q13 — Currency?**

➡️ A claim is single-currency by construction, so it batches under #229's one-currency rule with no new case. A claim whose currency is null (legacy) reads *Needs currency* as an undecidable reason — the same slot as *Needs bank details*.
