- **Exceptions' full-mode link differs from the other five**: `detailIdFor` maps a row to a *document*, not itself, and its "Open in a new tab" today goes to `documentBasePath`, not `basePath`. Give `QueueScreenProps` a `fullHref?: (row: T) => string` override (default `${basePath}/${(detailIdFor??rowId)(row)}?full=1`), and have `exception-queue.tsx` supply `fullHref={(row) => `${documentBasePath}/${row.documentId}?full=1`}`.
- **`DocumentDetailPage`'s `embedded` boolean param stays** (it decides whether the page wraps `SplitPane` in `PaneFrame mode="full"` for the standalone route, vs. being embedded already by the queue's own `PaneFrame`) — only `SplitPane`'s own `embedded` *prop* is deleted per spec §1 row 3. Don't conflate the two when editing.
