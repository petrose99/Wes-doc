## Decisions recorded
Supplier contacts are Owner-edited on Admin › Suppliers, pre-filled from the ledger but never written back. Remittance advice is sent only from a Payment record, only when the recording Owner chooses, once per record through an idempotent outbox, never corrected silently; bounces and missing contacts are visible states (ADR 0021).
