## Conventions the bar checks
One term per concept: "Ready to Approve" (phone h1), "Invoice approvals" / "PO mismatches" (segments), "Audit" (tab), the two offline/network sentences above. Every ⚠ action confirms with its consequence in the sheet ("This ends the approval run…"; "Approving moves it to ‹stage›" / "completes the Approval"). Keys: cards and segments in Tab order, ←/→ on tablists, ↑/↓ in Filter radiogroups, Escape innermost-only with the typed reason kept, focus returns to the card `<a>` on Back, the result strip takes focus. States: loading frame, "Nothing needs your approval" (+ "N waiting on other approvers" link), "No rows match these filters" + Clear filters, offline (submit disabled + reason), refused (strip), network error (sheet stays open, reason kept), deep link to a decided row (`initialMissingNotice`). Type scale below `lg`: h1 20px on 16px body; controls ≥ 44px.
