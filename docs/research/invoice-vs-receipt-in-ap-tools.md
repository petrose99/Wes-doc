# How AP and capture tools tell a supplier invoice from a receipt

The question: how do AP-automation and document-capture products separate a supplier **invoice / bill** (still owed, so it posts as a bill and is paid later) from a **receipt** (already paid, so it posts as an expense, spend money or purchase)? What do they call each side? Who decides the type? How do they handle the paid-by-card tax invoice? Can the type change after posting? What happens when both an invoice and its receipt arrive for one purchase? Facts only, researched 2026-09-26.

**Labels.** **Confirmed** means read on the vendor's own help centre, API reference or official schema in this pass. **Staff/forum** means an employee's answer on the vendor's own community site. **Unverified** means it comes from a search-engine summary, marketing copy, or inference. **Not found** means it was looked for in primary sources and no answer was found; the gap is not filled from memory.

## Summary

**The ledgers force the split.** Neither QuickBooks Online nor Xero has one record that can be either unpaid or paid.
- QBO has a **Bill** ("a transaction that you will pay in the future") and a paid **Expense / Check / Credit card expense**. In the API these are two entities: `Bill`, and `Purchase` with `PaymentType` Cash / Check / CreditCard.
- Xero has a **Bill** (API `Invoice` with `Type: ACCPAY`) and **Spend money** (API `BankTransaction` with `Type: SPEND`). Spend money is for "money you've spent that doesn't relate to a bill, expense claim or refund".

A tool that posts to both ledgers has to decide, per document, which record to create.

**There are three ways to paid-by-card.** A paid supplier document can reach the ledger as:
- **(a) a direct paid record**: Expense or Spend money, with no AP entry;
- **(b) a bill plus a payment**: the bill is created and immediately paid from a bank or card account;
- **(c) an attachment to a record that already exists**: the card transaction or bank feed line, with nothing new created.

Dext, AutoEntry and BILL expose (a) and (b) as a choice. Ramp, BILL Spend & Expense and Expensify use (c) for card spend. QBO's own Receipts tab does (a) or (c).

**Who decides the type varies a lot:**
- **Separate intake per type**: Ramp (`@ap.ramp.com` vs `receipts@ramp.com`), Expensify (`domain@expensify.cash` vs `receipts@expensify.com`), Xero's own Bills inbox vs mobile "Receipt".
- **Automatic classification, then a per-item override**: Dext, QBO, Ramp's AP inbox.
- **A payment field on each item that decides the posted record, not the document's look**: Dext's *Paid* toggle plus *Publish to*, AutoEntry's *Mark as Paid* plus *Publish To*, Hubdoc's *Publish As*.
- **Supplier or payment-method rules that pre-set that field**: Dext, AutoEntry, Hubdoc.

**The document type and the posting type are often separate fields.** Hubdoc and Dext both keep a descriptive **Document type** (Receipt, Invoice, Credit note, …) apart from the **publish type** (Purchase / Spend Money, or Bill / Cash / Check / Credit card). In both, the publish type is what picks the ledger record.

**Nothing un-posts.** No tool found converts a posted bill into an expense or the reverse. QBO itself cannot: Intuit's own answer is to delete one and re-enter the other. Hubdoc's re-publish updates the same transaction but "can't change the type". AutoEntry and Dext let you flip *paid* only before publishing.

**Duplicates are caught by field match inside each tool.** They are not reconciled across the invoice/receipt boundary:
- **Dext** keys receipts on supplier + date + total + owner, and invoices on supplier + total + reference.
- **Hubdoc** keys on date + supplier + total, plus the invoice number when present.
- **AutoEntry** checks the ledger for supplier + date + number + gross, and can attach the image to the existing record instead of posting again.
- **Expensify** merges a scanned receipt into a matching card transaction.
- **QBO** suggests matches to existing transactions.

No tool found automatically recognises "this receipt is the proof of payment for that bill" and records a bill payment from it. Ramp comes closest: a card transaction that paid a bill is matched to that bill and synced as a bill payment.

## Per-product notes

### Ramp

- **Separate intake by type. Confirmed.** Card receipts go to `receipts@ramp.com`; "Reimbursements must be forwarded to" a separate address ([Submitting receipts, memos, and accounting](https://support.ramp.com/submitting-receipts-memos-and-accounting-for-your-ramp-transactions)). Bills go to the company's `@ap.ramp.com` inbox. There, "Ramp processes emails from recognized employees and vendors, reviews supported attachments, creates draft bills from invoices and vendor credit memos … and filters out other files, decorative assets, exact duplicates" ([Bill Pay AP email forwarding](https://support.ramp.com/bill-pay-ap-email-forwarding)).
- **Classification setting. Confirmed.** *Create drafts from attachments* has two options. **Only if they appear to be invoices** (the default) "classifies attachments before creating drafts". **Always, regardless of attachment** creates a draft from each attachment (same page). A receipt sent to the AP inbox is therefore filtered out, not turned into a paid record.
- **The receipt side is the card transaction. Confirmed.** Ramp matches a receipt to a Ramp card transaction; the receipt is evidence, not a ledger record (receipts page above).
- **Paid-by-card bill. Confirmed.** "Once the card is swiped and the transaction clears, Ramp automatically matches the transaction and marks its payment paid." Then "Ramp will mark the matched payment as paid and sync over the card transaction as a bill payment … the AP liability is shifted to the credit card liability account" ([Pay Bill Pay invoices via Ramp card](https://support.ramp.com/pay-bill-pay-invoices-via-ramp-card)). For bills paid outside Ramp: "Mark as synced instead of retrying payment sync so Ramp does not attempt to create a duplicate bill payment" ([Bill Pay accounting](https://support.ramp.com/bill-pay-accounting)).
- **Change of type after sync:** not found.

### BILL (Bill.com) and BILL Spend & Expense

- **Inbox. Confirmed.** A person turns each Inbox document into a **Bill**, **Vendor credit** or **Payment received**, or attaches it to an existing record ("Add to existing"). "AI will try to detect and pre-populate some information for a bill." "If AI detects a potential duplicate bill or document in your inbox, we'll tag it" ([Process documents from the Inbox](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/4407875918349), updated 2026-09-17).
- **Paid-by-card receipt becomes bill + payment. Confirmed.** "Send each credit card receipt to your BILL Inbox → Enter bill … → Mark as paid → In the Chart of Account field, choose a credit card GL account". "The steps below work with all accounting system versions we sync with, except Xero." For Xero, BILL recommends "using BILL to track only Payables, and recording credit card charges in the accounting system", or storing the receipts as **Vendor Documents** ([Manage Credit Card Bills and Receipts](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/360000026086)). For a bill already entered and paid by card outside BILL, **Mark as paid** records an offline payment that syncs, and it "won't send a live payment to your vendor" ([Record a bill payment by credit card outside BILL](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/115005953606), 2026-09-22).
- **Spend & Expense is a separate product. Confirmed.** The sync matrix maps Receipt → Receipt and Reimbursement → **Bill**. "When transactions are synced from Spend & Expense, they display as credit card expenses in QuickBooks" ([QBO Sync Matrix — Spend & Expense](https://help.bill.com/direct/s/article/000002856)).

### Dext Prepare

- **One Costs inbox, three fields. Confirmed.** The item page has **Type** ("for example, receipt or invoice"). "Due date is only available for invoice document types." The **Payment** section holds the *Paid* toggle, **Payment method**, **Publish to**, and **Publish as** (Xero only) ([Item details page](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/105676-how-to-use-the-item-details-page-in-dext)). Extraction fills in **Document type** ("receipt, invoice, or credit note") and **Payment method**: "the last 4 digits of the card used for the payment (for Costs documents only)" ([What information does Dext extract](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416691-what-information-does-dext-extract-from-costs-and-sales-documents)).
- **Xero destinations. Confirmed.** **Purchases** ("for unpaid and paid bills"), **Classic Expense Claims**, and **Bank Accounts** ("for expenses already paid from a business bank account. Items publish as Spend Money transactions"). Publish statuses to Purchases: Draft / Awaiting Approval / Awaiting Payment / **Paid (where possible)**. "To publish … to Purchases as Paid, the payment method assigned to the item must be linked to a Xero bank account. Without a linked bank account, Dext will automatically publish the item as Awaiting Payment instead." "Publishing to Bank Accounts does not create an invoice in Xero. If you need an invoice and want to reconcile it later, publish to Purchases as Paid" ([Publish documents to Xero](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/377055-how-to-publish-documents-to-xero)).
- **QBO destinations. Confirmed.** *Publish to* **Cash, Check, or Credit card** → "appears in Expenses in QuickBooks Online as a paid transaction". **Bill** → "a bill waiting to be paid". A default is set "in the connection settings", "per item", or "as a supplier rule" ([Publish to QuickBooks Online](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/377056-how-to-publish-cost-and-sales-items-from-dext-to-quickbooks-online)). A search excerpt of the same page also says an item "marked as paid" but left on Bill "will be sent to Bill but will appear as unpaid in QBO" (unverified wording; the page read did not show it).
- **Rules. Confirmed.** Supplier rules set "payment method, … paid status — as well as publishing behaviour". Payment-method rules set a default **Publish to**, a linked **Bank account** and **Auto-publish** "to all Cost items paid using that method" ([Supplier and customer rules](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/216125-how-to-use-supplier-and-customer-rules-in-dext); [Payment method rules](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416719-how-to-set-up-payment-method-rules-in-dext), 2026-08-20). The effect is that a card slip whose last-4 matches a company card can be routed to that card's account and destination with no human choice.
- **Duplicates. Confirmed.** "For receipts: Supplier, Date, Total amount, Document owner." "For invoices and credit notes: Supplier, Total amount, Document reference." Modes are Automatic (delete), Review (amber flag) or Off, set per account or per supplier. Costs only ([How Dext handles duplicate Cost documents](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/216124-how-dext-handles-duplicate-cost-documents), 2026-07-30). An invoice and its receipt have different keys, so they are compared only if both are typed the same way (inference).
- **Change after publishing:** not found.

### Hubdoc (Xero)

- **Publish As picks the record. Confirmed.** On a Xero business edition: **Purchase**, **Spend Money**, **Invoice (AR)**, **Credit Note**. On partner edition, Spend Money only. Purchase "create[s] a bill … for bills that you have to pay by a certain date", with status Draft / Awaiting Approval / Awaiting Payment. "Select Spend Money if you pay for a purchase straight away … creates a spend money transaction in the bank account used for the purchase" ([About publishing documents to Xero](https://central.xero.com/s/article/About-publishing-documents-to-Xero-UK)).
- **No change of type on re-publish. Confirmed.** Re-publish "updates the transaction initially published in Xero … It doesn't create a new transaction". "You can't re-publish a document if … The initial transaction created in Xero is deleted, voided, edited, reconciled, or paid." "When you re-publish a document to Xero, you can't change the type of transaction the document was published as (Publish As field) or the Status field" (same page).
- **Document type is only descriptive. Confirmed.** Document Type options are Invoice, Statement, Report, Receipt, CSV, Check, Deposit, eTransfer, Invoice (AR), Payment, Credit Memo, Purchase Order, Other. "Receipt: A document acknowledging a payment you've made." The page also says of **Mark as Paid**: "For your reference … Marking a document as paid doesn't affect how it's published to Xero, or its status in Xero" ([About data extraction in Hubdoc](https://central.xero.com/s/article/About-data-extraction-in-Hubdoc-US-SG-SA-ROW)).
- **Duplicates. Confirmed.** A duplicate has "the same date, supplier name, and total amount … Two documents must have the same invoice numbers". The user chooses *Not a duplicate* or *Move to Trash*. Detection can be turned off per supplier. Duplicates from an autosync supplier "aren't automatically published" (same page).
- **QBO.** Publishes as bill, expense or supplier credit, per search excerpts of Hubdoc pages. **Unverified**: the Hubdoc QBO article was not found or read.

### AutoEntry

- **Paid is a toggle on a purchase invoice. Confirmed.** "By default, when you publish an invoice, it will post it in your accounting software as outstanding / unpaid." With *Mark as Paid*, it posts "directly as paid, and record[s] the transaction in the bank". It can be set manually per invoice or as a **supplier default**. **Publish To** is either "the payment directly without invoice, or against the supplier account. The latter will publish both the invoice and the payment." For Xero: *Purchases* (invoice + payment) or *Bank account* (bank payment only). "You can toggle off the paid feature from an individual invoice anytime before you publish it" ([Mark purchase invoices as 'paid'](https://help.autoentry.com/en/articles/5029312-mark-purchase-invoices-as-paid), 2026-01-26). No separate "receipt" document type was found; receipts go through the Purchase folder.
- **Duplicates against the ledger. Confirmed.** After publishing, AutoEntry checks "existing invoices in your accounting software" on supplier, invoice date, invoice number and gross total. It then offers **Attach** ("only attaches the image … No edits or updates are made to the existing data"), **Publish** or **Reject**. On QBO, "Matches will be suggested for Expenses without a reference number … to facilitate File Matching of Expenses added directly from a Bank Statement via QuickBooks' Banking module" ([File Matching](https://help.autoentry.com/en/articles/2998187-file-match-xero-and-quickbooks-online), 2025-07-09).

### Vic.ai

- Invoice-only AP. For a document that is not a bill: "The transferred icon means that your bill has been sent to your connected payables system/accounting system inbox without creating a bill object or a journal entry. Typically transferring is used to send a document to your connected system that isn't a bill" ([Vic.ai FAQ](https://intercom.help/vicai/en/articles/3720674-faq-you-have-questions-vic-ai-has-answers)). **Confirmed.** Receipt or expense handling: not found.

### Stampli

- Invoice-only AP. A receipt is a **supporting document**: "Add a supporting document when the invoice itself is correct and you are attaching extra evidence around it, such as a contract, packing slip, receipt, or approval email." "A statement is not a payable document" ([Add a supporting document vs replace the invoice](https://www.stampli.com/resources/add-or-replace-invoice-document/), Stampli's own reference page, not the help centre). Help-centre articles need sign-in ([Emailing invoices to Stampli](https://help.stampli.com/en/articles/5581626-emailing-invoices-to-stampli) returned "Unable to load this article, you may need to sign in first"). Card spend runs through Stampli Card ([Stampli Cards](https://www.stampli.com/card/), marketing). Paid-invoice and duplicate handling: not found.

### Tipalti

- AP bills and **Tipalti Expenses** are separate products (Tipalti marketing, **unverified**). **Mark as paid manually**: the user chooses the "Bank" or "Credit Card" account that paid it, and "the GL account type (Bank/Credit card) and transaction date … are synced to the payer's ERP under the relevant bill payment" ([How do I manually mark bills as paid?](https://help.tipalti.com/hc/en-us/articles/26573049765399-How-do-I-manually-mark-bills-as-paid)). **Unverified**: the text comes from a search excerpt, because the page returned 403 to the fetcher and rendered only navigation in a browser.

### Yooz

- Marketing says Yooz captures "invoices, PR, PO, credit notes, goods receipt" with "automatic capture and classification of any document type" and offers T&E automation ([getyooz.com](https://www.getyooz.com/product)). **Unverified**; no help-centre article on invoice vs receipt was found.

### Expensify

- **Bills vs expenses by intake. Confirmed.** "Forward bills to `domain.com@expensify.cash`." "A bill represents a payable amount owed to a vendor"; once approved "it can be exported back to your accounting system" ([Receive and Pay Bills](https://help.expensify.com/articles/expensify-classic/bank-accounts-and-payments/payments/Receive-and-Pay-Bills)). Receipts are SmartScanned into **expenses** (receipts@expensify.com, per search excerpts, **unverified**).
- **Export by reimbursable flag. Confirmed.** Reimbursable → "Vendor Bills (recommended)", Checks, or Journal Entries. Non-reimbursable → "Credit Card Expenses … Exported individually as bank transactions", Debit Card, or Vendor Bills ([Configure QuickBooks Online](https://help.expensify.com/articles/expensify-classic/connections/quickbooks-online/Configure-Quickbooks-Online)). Workspaces can make cash expenses reimbursable or non-reimbursable by default, or force non-reimbursable (search excerpt of [Expense Settings](https://help.expensify.com/articles/expensify-classic/workspaces/Expense-Settings), **unverified**).
- **Receipt ↔ card-transaction merge.** SmartScan merges a receipt with a card transaction that matches closely on amount, date and currency. If the card transaction has not posted within seven days, they stay as two expenses that can be merged by hand ([Why Expenses Duplicate](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Why-Expenses-Duplicate); **unverified**, search excerpt).

### Xero (native)

- **Records. Confirmed (API).** Bill = `Invoice` with `Type: ACCPAY`. Spend money = `BankTransaction` with `Type` enum RECEIVE, RECEIVE-OVERPAYMENT, RECEIVE-PREPAYMENT, SPEND, SPEND-OVERPAYMENT, SPEND-PREPAYMENT ([xero_accounting.yaml](https://github.com/XeroAPI/Xero-OpenAPI/blob/master/xero_accounting.yaml)).
- **Entry point decides. Confirmed.** Uploading on the Bills screen or emailing the bills address creates a **draft bill** each time ([Upload bills](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Upload-bills-into-Xero); [Email bills](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Email-PDF-bills-into-your-Xero-organisation)). Spend money records "money you've spent that doesn't relate to a bill, expense claim or refund". On mobile it is created with **+ → Receipt** ([Create a spend money transaction](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Add-a-spend-money-transaction)). In **Xero Expenses**, *How did you pay?* → **Company money (non-reimbursable)** + a company bank account. "When an expense paid with company money is approved, Xero automatically creates a spend money transaction for you to reconcile" ([Process an expense paid with company money](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Expenses-paid-with-company-money)).
- Converting a bill to spend money, or the reverse: not found. Duplicate handling in the bills inbox: not found in the articles read.

Unverified (forum).** Community answers say a bill cannot be converted to an expense; delete it and re-enter ([Can you convert a bill to an expense?](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/reports-and-accounting/can-you-convert-a-bill-to-an-expense/00/467989)).

### Zahara

## Patterns

1. **Two intakes (Ramp, Expensify, Xero native).** Invoices go to an AP inbox and become bills; receipts go to a receipts channel and attach to an existing card transaction, or become an expense or claim. The channel asserts the type. A document sent to the wrong channel is filtered out (Ramp) or has to be re-sent. This works because card spend already exists as a record from the card feed.
2. **One inbox + payment fields that pick the record (Dext, AutoEntry, Hubdoc, BILL).** The document type is informational (and, in Dext and QBO, auto-detected). A *Paid* / *Payment method* / *Publish to* (or *Publish As*) choice decides bill, bill + payment, or direct expense. Supplier and payment-method rules make that choice automatic for repeat suppliers and company cards.
3. **Auto-classify, human confirms (QBO Receipts, Ramp AP inbox, BILL Inbox AI).** The machine guesses; the review step shows the guess and allows an override before anything posts. QBO's criteria are undocumented, and users ask how to force a type.
4. **Invoice-only AP (Vic.ai, Stampli, Tipalti AP, Zahara AP).** Receipts are not an AP document. They are supporting evidence, transferred, or left to a separate expense product. A paid invoice is still a bill, closed with a **mark as paid / offline payment** that syncs a bill payment against a bank or card account.
5. **Type is fixed once posted, everywhere.** Changes happen before publish (AutoEntry, Dext) or not at all (Hubdoc re-publish, QBO).

**Fit for DocuBite** (CONTEXT.md: the user asserts the typed destination at upload and that assertion is authoritative; email and WhatsApp default to Receipts; re-type with *Move to another queue*; Invoice posts as a bill, Receipt as an expense; nothing un-posts). DocuBite is closest to **pattern 1's channel-asserts-type** combined with **pattern 5**. The tools most like it on the receipt side are Hubdoc and Dext, and both add something DocuBite has no equivalent for: a paid choice on the invoice side. The paid-by-card tax invoice is the case where their designs give the most useful precedent:

- **Keep it an Invoice, record it as paid.** This is Dext "Purchases as Paid", AutoEntry "Purchases" and BILL "Mark as paid" to a card GL account: a bill plus a payment, which keeps AP history per supplier. DocuBite's Bank Statement match ("Confirm paid") already records *Paid* with evidence, but only after the fact and never as a ledger payment.
- **Move it to Receipts, post as a direct expense.** This is Dext "Bank Accounts" and Hubdoc "Spend Money": simpler, but the supplier's bill history then has a gap.

Which of these to offer is a decision, not a finding. All the tools that keep a separate document type field also keep the posting choice separate from it, and none of them converts after posting. That supports DocuBite's rule that re-typing is allowed only before posting.

Duplicate precedents worth noting:
- Dext keys receipts and invoices differently, so an invoice and its receipt are unlikely to collide (inference).
- AutoEntry checks against the **ledger**, including QBO bank-feed expenses with no reference, and offers *Attach* instead of posting twice.
- Expensify merges a receipt into its card transaction.

None of them links an invoice to its later receipt as proof of payment. Ramp comes nearest, through card transaction ↔ bill matching.
