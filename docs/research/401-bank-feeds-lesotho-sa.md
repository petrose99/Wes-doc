# Bank feeds into QBO / Xero / Sage for Lesotho & South Africa — facts for a design decision

Ticket: research support for a Wayfinder decision on whether DocuBite should build its own "post a Bank Statement's lines into the ledger" feature. This document is facts only — primary sources where reachable, search-engine synthesis (marked as such) where a vendor page could not be fetched directly. It is **not** a recommendation to build.

## Question

Do Standard Lesotho Bank, FNB Lesotho, Nedbank Lesotho, or Lesotho PostBank have a direct (automatic-sync) bank feed into QuickBooks Online, Xero, or Sage Business Cloud Accounting? Where there is no direct feed, how do bookkeepers get bank lines in today, and does each ledger's API let an external app (like DocuBite, via Nango) create a bank transaction/statement line programmatically?

## Summary / Answer

**No direct bank feed exists for any Lesotho bank into any of the three ledgers.** None of QuickBooks Online's, Xero's, or Sage's own supported-bank pages list a Lesotho institution (LSL currency, "Lesotho," or the four named banks do not appear); South African feed coverage stops at the major SA banks. This was expected and is confirmed rather than assumed, per the findings below.

For South Africa, direct feeds exist but are inconsistent and thin: QuickBooks online.za lists exactly 6 SA banks; Xero's own direct-feed articles show a bank-by-bank patchwork (Investec and Nedbank confirmed via dedicated Xero Central pages; Standard Bank appears to run through a third-party setup guide rather than a native Xero feed); Sage claims feeds from the 5 major SA banks. **Capitec and full Lesotho coverage are not confirmed as direct feeds anywhere found.**

Where there's no feed, the standard path today is manual statement import (CSV/QBO/QFX into QuickBooks; CSV/OFX into Xero and Sage) — a solved, well-documented, native capability in all three ledgers, not a gap. The gap, if any, is narrower than "get bank lines into the ledger": it's specifically for **Lesotho banks that don't produce a downloadable CSV/OFX at all**, or for firms that want statement lines to arrive automatically rather than via manual upload/paste.

On the API question, the picture differs by ledger and matters for what DocuBite could build:

- **Xero**: the public Accounting API's `BankTransactions` endpoint lets an external app POST transactions (SPEND/RECEIVE) directly against a bank account — but these post as already-categorized, effectively-reconciled transactions, not as unreconciled statement lines awaiting matching. The endpoint that actually posts statement lines for reconciliation, the **Bank Feeds API**, is closed to accredited financial-institution partners only — Xero's own developer-idea tracker confirms Xero does not support posting/reconciling raw statement lines via the public API and has no plan to add it.
- **QuickBooks Online**: `Purchase`, `Deposit`, and `BankTransfer` entities can all be created via the API against a bank account, but — per Intuit's own developer community — these post as regular, already-recorded transactions; an app **cannot** post into the bank's "For Review" (OFX-derived) queue that a native feed populates.
- **Sage Business Cloud Accounting API (v3.1)**: `bank_transactions` (money in / money out, e.g. `OTHER_PAYMENT`) support POST/create per Sage's own developer docs' response-code table, but this creates a recorded bank transaction, not a feed-style statement line either.

**Conclusion for the decision**: a DocuBite feature that posts a Bank Statement's lines as *already-created transactions* (Xero BankTransactions / QBO Purchase-Deposit / Sage bank_transactions) is technically buildable on all three and *would* fill a real gap specifically for Lesotho banks and any unfed SA bank, because no direct feed reaches those ledgers there. It would **not** replicate what a native feed does (arrival in an unreconciled "for review"/reconcile queue for matching) on any of the three ledgers — that path is either partner-only (Xero Bank Feeds API) or not exposed at all (QBO, Sage) to third-party apps. Whether that distinction matters is a product decision Intent should weigh, not an API limitation DocuBite can currently route around.
