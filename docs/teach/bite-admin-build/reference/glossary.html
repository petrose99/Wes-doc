Organization, Company, Role

The four words that carry the whole admin layer. Source: CONTEXT.md lines 155–170. Print this.

WordIsNever called
CompanyOne set of books: a team workspace. What Bill Pay, Invoices, Approvals all belong to.workspace (on screen), entity, tenant
PersonalThe one workspace every user gets for themselves. Never inside an organization.personal workspace, home
OrganizationA named group of companies one firm runs under one login. Belonging to it opens no company.firm, account, tenant
RoleYour standing in one company: Owner, Reviewer or Member. Per company; the only thing that decides what you can do there.permission level, access level
Organization adminA single flag on a person, shown as a fact or “—”. Lets you rename the org and see it in Admin. Opens no company.Org admin, organization member, organization owner
AdminThe area at the bottom of the rail. Organization section (Dashboard, Companies, Users) + current Company section (Configuration, flows, Suppliers, Integrations).Settings, Controls, administrator (as a role)
Three admin-ish words and no fourth: Organization admin (the org), Owner (a company), Admin (the area). The platform operator's User.role = "admin" is never shown.
