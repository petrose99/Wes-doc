The Companies page

Admin › Companies is the list of drawers in your cabinet. It looks different depending on which drawer you are standing in when you open it. This lesson is the three looks, and the one action it refuses.

Three looks, decided by where you are

The page reads the company you are currently in (the URL's /workspaces/[id]/…) and picks one of three states (admin/companies/page.tsx, lines 24–31):

You are in…You seeWhy
your Personal workspacea first-use screen: Create a companyPersonal is never in an organization, so there is nothing to list; the only sensible next step is to start a company.
a company that is not yet in an organizationa first-use screen: Name your organizationOne company alone does not need a cabinet. The moment you name one, this company becomes its first member and you its first Organization admin.
a company inside an organizationthe real list: every company in that organization you belong to, one row each, a pane per rowThis is the working state.
Rows only for companies you belong to. If the organization has more, a small line under the list says "3 more in Acme you're not a member of" (companies-queue.tsx line 265). That is the Lesson 1 rule made visible: the organization knows the drawers exist, but you don't get to open them.

What you can do (Owners only)

  • Add a company — creates a new company straight into this organization.
  • Move a company into Acme — picks from companies you own that are not in any organization yet, and pulls one in. You must own both sides.
  • Remove from Acme (in a row's ⋯ menu) — takes the company out of the organization. The company keeps existing, it just stands alone again.

A Reviewer or Member sees the list and the panes but none of these buttons.

The one refusal

You cannot remove the company you are standing in.

The menu item is disabled with the hint "Switch to another company to remove this one." — and the server refuses it too (companies/actions.ts line 172, error is_current). Reason: removing your current company would pull the floor out from under the page you are on. The product glossary makes that caption the only place the rule is stated; no other screen offers the action.

Look at it

On the tunnel URL: open Admin › Companies from a company that is in an organization. Open a row's ⋯ menu — if it is the company you are in, Remove is greyed with the hint.
