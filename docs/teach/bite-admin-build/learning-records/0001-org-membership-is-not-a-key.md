# Organization membership is not a key to its companies

The learner can state, in their own words, that a person in an organization gets nothing in a company until given a role in that company, and gave the reason themselves: a firm wants an employee in some client companies but not others under the same organization. This is the central rule of the admin layer; future lessons can build on it (per-company invites on Users, the Companies "switch first" rule) without re-teaching it.

## Evidence
Answered "nothing, he needs a role in that company first" and, for why the org has no member rank, "sometimes in an organization we want an employee to be part of certain companies but not the other under the same org" (2026-09-19).
