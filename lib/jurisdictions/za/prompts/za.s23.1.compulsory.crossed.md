# za.s23.1.compulsory.crossed

Compulsory VAT registration is triggered when taxable supplies exceed
**R1,000,000** in any 12-month period (VAT Act s23(1)). The Budget 2026 threshold rises to
**R2,300,000** on **1 April 2026** — SARS FAQ:
<https://www.sars.gov.za/faq/what-is-the-new-threshold-for-vat-registration/> and
<https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/>.

The rule row picks the threshold in force at the supply date so a 5-year lookback stays
correct across the jump.
