# za.s20.4.e.description-and-quantity

Full tax invoices require an accurate description of the goods/services **and** the quantity
or volume supplied — VAT Act s20(4)(e); SARS:
<https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/government/tax-invoices/>.

Abridged invoices (≤ R5,000, s20(5)) drop the quantity/volume line but keep the description.
