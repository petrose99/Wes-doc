# za.s20.4.a.tax-invoice-wording

A ZA tax invoice must carry the words **"Tax Invoice"**, **"VAT Invoice"** or **"Invoice"** —
VAT Act 89 of 1991, s20(4)(a). SARS restates this in its tax-invoices requirements page:
<https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/government/tax-invoices/>.

If the wording is missing, the document is not a valid tax invoice under s20 and input VAT
under s16(2)(a) may not be claimed against it. Ask the supplier to reissue.
