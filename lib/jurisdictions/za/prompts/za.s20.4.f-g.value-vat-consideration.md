# za.s20.4.f-g.value-vat-consideration

The invoice must show either the value, VAT amount and consideration separately, **or** state
that the consideration includes VAT together with the rate applied (15%) — VAT Act s20(4)(f)–(g);
SARS: <https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/government/tax-invoices/>.
