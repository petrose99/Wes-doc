# za.s20.4.c.recipient-identity

For a full tax invoice (consideration > R5,000), recipient name and address are required, plus
the recipient's VAT registration number if the recipient is itself a registered vendor —
VAT Act s20(4)(c); SARS: <https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/government/tax-invoices/>.

Abridged invoices (≤ R5,000, s20(5)) do not require recipient details.
