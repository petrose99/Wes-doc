# za.s20.4.currency-zar

A ZA tax invoice must be denominated in South African Rand — VAT Act s20; VAT404 Guide for
Vendors, Issue 15, ch. 13: <https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-Pub-Guide-VAT404-VAT-404-Guide-for-Vendors.pdf>.

Zero-rated supplies under s11 (e.g. exports) may be in foreign currency; all other supplies
must be in ZAR.
